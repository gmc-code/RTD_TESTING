==========================
Australian Desert Food Web
==========================

The deserts of central Australia are hot and dry, with red sandy soil and
rain that can stay away for months or even years. Spinifex grass, mulga
trees and saltbush survive the dry times, and after rain the ground is
covered in wildflowers. These plants feed termites, grasshoppers, mice,
finches and kangaroos, and those feed lizards, snakes, cats, dingoes and
eagles.

A food web joins all the food chains in a community together. Each arrow
points from the food to the organism that eats it, so the arrows show which
way the energy goes.

Build the food web
==================

The four producers are already in place. Drag each animal from the tray into
the right box, then press **Check**. Tap a picture (or start dragging it) and
the box at the top of the tray says what that animal eats. The arrows are
clues too: look at what goes into and out of each box.

**Explore** shows the finished web: point at (or tap) an organism to see what
it eats and what eats it. **Trace a chain** builds a food chain by tapping the
organisms in order.

Food chains
===========

There are **30 food chains** in this web. Every chain ends at the dingo, the
wedge-tailed eagle or the bilby. The shortest chains have three organisms and
the longest have five.

Checking the feeding relationships
==================================

Every link in the food web was checked against the sources listed at the
bottom.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 30 50

   * - Organism
     - Eats (in this web)
     - What the sources say
   * - Termite
     - Spinifex grass, mulga
     - Spinifex termites build mud tubes along spinifex blades to feed on
       them, and harvester termites store grass in their nests. In mulga
       country, termites feed on dead wood, leaf litter and dead grass.
   * - Grasshopper
     - Saltbush, desert wildflowers
     - Saltbush country supports huge numbers of plant-eating insects such
       as grasshoppers. Grasshoppers eat grasses, leaves and stems.
   * - House mouse
     - Spinifex grass, desert wildflowers
     - Eats mainly seeds and grains, as well as shoots and insects.
   * - Zebra finches
     - Spinifex grass
     - Zebra finches eat mainly grass seeds, including the seeds of native
       grasses such as spinifex.
   * - Red kangaroo
     - Desert wildflowers, saltbush
     - Mainly eats green grasses and leafy plants (forbs). When grass is
       short it eats saltbush and other shrubs.
   * - Wolf spider
     - Grasshopper
     - A ground hunter that chases small invertebrates such as grasshoppers,
       crickets and other spiders.
   * - Bearded dragon
     - Termite, grasshopper, desert wildflowers
     - An omnivore. Adults eat mostly flowers, leaves and fruit, plus
       insects such as termites and grasshoppers.
   * - Bilby
     - Termite, wolf spider
     - Termites, beetles, insect larvae and spiders are the animals it eats
       most often. It also eats seeds, bulbs and fungi.
   * - Brown snake
     - House mouse, zebra finches, bearded dragon
     - Western brown snakes eat lizards, other snakes, young birds and eggs,
       and small mammals, especially house mice.
   * - Perentie
     - House mouse, bearded dragon
     - Eats other reptiles (including central bearded dragons), small
       mammals and birds. It is an important predator of house mice.
   * - Dingo
     - Red kangaroo, perentie
     - Red kangaroos and euros make up most of the dingo's food in arid
       Western Australia. In the deserts of South Australia dingoes ate
       kangaroos and large reptiles.
   * - Wedge-tailed eagle
     - Red kangaroo, perentie, brown snake
     - Takes young kangaroos, goannas (including perenties) and
       brown snakes. It also eats carrion such as dead kangaroos.

.. note::

   Some links are left out to keep the web readable. Bilbies also eat
   grasshoppers, seeds and bulbs, wolf spiders also eat termites,
   perenties also eat small marsupials such as bilbies,
   eagles also eat bearded dragons, and dingoes and eagles also
   feed on dead kangaroos. Scavenging and decomposers are left out of this
   web.
