======================================
Arctic Tundra Food Chains and Food Web
======================================

The Arctic tundra is cold, windy and treeless. The ground below the surface
stays frozen all year (permafrost), so only low-growing plants survive. Even
so, a community of plants and animals lives there, all linked by what they
eat.

Every food chain on this page starts at a plant and ends at an animal that
nothing else in this community eats. The arrow means *is eaten by*.

Checking the feeding relationships
==================================

Each card in the cut-out pack says what the organism eats. Every link used on
this page was checked against the sources listed at the bottom.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 22 30 48

   * - Organism
     - Card says it eats
     - What the sources say
   * - Woolly bear caterpillar
     - Willow leaves and saxifrage
     - Arctic willow is its main food plant: it eats the buds
       and young leaves in early summer. It also eats purple saxifrage.
   * - Brown lemming
     - Cottongrass, moss and sedges
     - Brown lemmings eat mostly grasses and sedges in summer
       and more moss in winter. Cottongrass (a sedge) is one of its foods.
   * - Arctic hare
     - Woody twigs, bark and willow roots
     - Arctic willow is its main food all year. Its leaves,
       buds, bark and roots make up nearly all of the winter diet.
   * - Ptarmigan
     - Seeds, berries, willow buds and caterpillars
     - Adults eat buds, catkins, leaves, berries and seeds,
       including willow. Chicks need protein and eat many insects, especially
       caterpillars.
   * - Snowy owl
     - Lemmings, voles and ptarmigan
     - Lemmings are by far its main food. It also takes voles,
       ptarmigan and young hares.
   * - Arctic fox
     - Lemmings, caterpillars, bird eggs and hares
     - Lemmings are its main food. It also eats bird eggs,
       birds, young hares and insects. (Sources say *insects* in general
       rather than woolly bear caterpillars by name.)
   * - Gray wolf
     - Hares, lemmings and ptarmigan
     - Arctic wolves mainly hunt muskoxen and Arctic hares,
       and also eat lemmings, ptarmigan and other birds.

.. note::

   Some foods in the sources are left out of the food web because they are
   not in this community: saxifrage, voles, muskoxen, caribou and berries.
   Sources also say snowy owls sometimes catch young hares and that wolves
   sometimes kill Arctic foxes. Those links are not on the cards, so they are
   not drawn here.

Food chains
===========

There are **12 food chains** in this community: 3 start at Cottongrass and 9
start at Arctic willow. The longest chains have four organisms.

Starting at Cottongrass (3)
---------------------------

#. Cottongrass → Brown lemming → Snowy owl
#. Cottongrass → Brown lemming → Arctic fox
#. Cottongrass → Brown lemming → Gray wolf

Starting at Arctic willow (9)
-----------------------------

#. Arctic willow → Arctic hare → Arctic fox
#. Arctic willow → Arctic hare → Gray wolf
#. Arctic willow → Ptarmigan → Snowy owl
#. Arctic willow → Ptarmigan → Arctic fox
#. Arctic willow → Ptarmigan → Gray wolf
#. Arctic willow → Woolly bear caterpillar → Arctic fox
#. Arctic willow → Woolly bear caterpillar → Ptarmigan → Snowy owl
#. Arctic willow → Woolly bear caterpillar → Ptarmigan → Arctic fox
#. Arctic willow → Woolly bear caterpillar → Ptarmigan → Gray wolf

The food web
============

A food web joins all the food chains in a community together. Each arrow
points from the food to the organism that eats it, so the arrows show which
way the energy goes.

Point at (or tap) an organism to see what it eats and what eats it. Press
**Trace a chain** to build a food chain by tapping the organisms in order.

.. foodweb:: Arctic tundra food web
   :images: Arctic_tundra/{name}.png
   :theme: ocean
   :apex: top
   :level-labels: Producers, Herbivores, Omnivore, Carnivores

   Woolly bear caterpillar eats Arctic willow
   Brown lemming eats Cottongrass
   Arctic hare eats Arctic willow
   Ptarmigan eats Arctic willow, Woolly bear caterpillar
   Snowy owl eats Brown lemming, Ptarmigan
   Arctic fox eats Brown lemming, Woolly bear caterpillar, Ptarmigan (eggs), Arctic hare
   Gray wolf eats Arctic hare, Brown lemming, Ptarmigan

   .. levels::

      Snowy owl, Arctic fox, Gray wolf
      Ptarmigan
      Woolly bear caterpillar, Arctic hare, Brown lemming
      Arctic willow, Cottongrass

Questions
---------

#. Name the two producers in this food web.
#. Which animal is eaten by the most other animals? What would happen to the
   snowy owl, Arctic fox and gray wolf if that animal disappeared?
#. The ptarmigan is labelled an omnivore. Use the food web to explain why.
#. Arctic willow is in 9 of the 12 food chains. Explain why it is so
   important to this community.
